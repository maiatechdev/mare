## Direction contract

THESIS: Every report becomes a numbered, stamped protocol entry in the colônia's register book: the R in M.A.R.E made physical. Refuses the split startup hero over a row of equal cards and the dark-ocean neon dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white ruled register paper (never cream) with faint teal rules and one red margin line that runs down the whole page as its only rail; dark bands in the green-black of the livro ata cover. Three inks: paper, stamp blue, buoy orange. Condensed stamp lettering for display, typewriter for protocol numbers and sources, a hyperlegible sans for reading. Stamps are rotated, slightly uneven ink; done is a solid rule, planned is dashed; missing content is a blank ruled line, never invented text.

STORY: The visitor understands in five seconds that fishers report erosion by WhatsApp and M.A.R.E turns it into an auditable record; believes it through the live protocol, sourced numbers and the honest stamped estimates; acts by reading the five steps or contacting the team.
